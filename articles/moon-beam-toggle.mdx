## I built a dark mode toggle
This was a small UI experiment.

I wanted it to feel a bit more atmospheric than a standard switch, so when it changes state it turns into a moonlight beam. The idea was to keep it simple, soft, and playful, with most of the work going into the motion, lighting, and overall feel.
